EPI-Summary
§ A total of 5,607 suspected cases have been reported from nine (9) States since the onset of the
outbreak in January 2018
§ Bauchi State has reported 3,757 cases which is the highest in the country so far, followed by
Adamawa 484 cases, Yobe with 449 cases, Borno with 429 cases, Kano with 339 cases, Plateau
with 70 cases and Zamfara with 48 cases.
§ Of the total 5,607 suspected cases reported, 98 were laboratory confirmed
§ The overall Case Fatality Rate (CFR) for all cases was 1.05%
§ Most affected age groups are 5-14 years (24.8%) and 1-4years (23.4%) while Male-female ratio
is 1.2:1
