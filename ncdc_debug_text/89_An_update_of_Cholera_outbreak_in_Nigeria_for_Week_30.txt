CHOLERA OUTBREAK IN NIGERIA
TITLE:
SERIAL NUMBER: 09
EPI-WEEK: 29
DATE: 24 July 2018
HIGHLIGHTS
 The multi-agency cholera emergency operations centre (EOC) at NCDC continues to coordinate
the response.
 There is a general decline in the number of cases reported. However, cases are still being
reported from seven States; Adamawa, Bauchi, Kaduna, Katsina, Niger, Kano and Zamfara.
 No cases reported in the following states in the last two weeks: Gombe, FCT, Jigawa, Kogi,
Yobe, Borno, Anambra and Nasarawa.
EPI-Summary
 In week 29 (16-22 July); 377 new suspected cases were reported from seven states- Adamawa
(19), Bauchi (10), Kaduna (32), Kano (1), Katsina (81), Niger (6) and Zamfara (228) including nine
deaths in four States Bauchi (1), Kaduna (4), Katsina (1), Zamfara (3).
 As at 24th July 2018, a total of 17,897 suspected cases including 223 deaths (CFR: 1.25%) have
been reported from 17 States (Adamawa, Anambra, Bauchi, Borno, Ebonyi, FCT, Gombe, Jigawa,
Kaduna, Kano, Katsina, Kogi, Nasarawa, Niger, Plateau, Yobe and Zamfara) since the beginning of
2018.
 Increasing number of cases reported in Zamfara, Kaduna and Katsina states.
 51.9 % of the cases are aged 1-14 years; 1-4years (27.1%) and 5-14years (24.8%)
 49.8% male and 50.2% female affected

RESPONSE ACTIVITIES
Coordination
 Response is being coordinated by NCDC through an EOC in collaboration with NPHCDA, Federal
Ministry of Water Resources (FMWR), WHO, UNICEF, MSF, AFENET, UMB and US-CDC
 EOC meetings hold in most affected states to coordinate the outbreak response
 National cholera stakeholders’ consensus meeting held to discuss cholera hotspot LGAs and
review of preventive OCV campaign implementation schedule on Thursday 19th July, 2018
Surveillance
 Clinician sensitization for early detection and reporting conducted
 Active case search is ongoing in affected States
…PROTECTING THE HEALTH OF NIGERIANS
 Cases are included in the line-list daily with intensified surveillance in high risk states
Case management
 Cases are being treated at designated health facilities or Cholera Treatment Centres (CTCs) in the
affected States supported by partners
WaSH
 Hygiene promotion and community sensitization ongoing
Laboratory Surveillance
 Samples are being tested with Rapid Diagnostic Test kits (RDTs) in the field and confirmed by stool
culture.
 Assessment and mapping of two labs per states ongoing in Plateau, Katsina, Kano and Kaduna states
 Total number of positive laboratory results: 288
Risk communications
 Radio jingles in English, Hausa and pidgin ongoing in affected states
 IEC materials distribution to 10 states and NPHCDA
Logistics and Vaccination
 First round of OCV campaign completed in Maiha, Mubi North and Mubi South LGAs of Adamawa
State vaccinating 377,935 persons with an administrative coverage of 112%
 Second round scheduled for 11-15 August, 2018
CHALLENGES
 Delay in submission of line-list for national update by some states
 Inadequate number of trained health care workers for case management
 Low proportion of samples tested by culture
 Poor IPC practices in health facilities
NEXT STEPS
 Deploy national RRTs to states with increasing number of cases
 Conduct laboratory needs assessment for cholera diagnosis in all the cholera active states
 Deploy risk communications team to Zamfara states for care givers sensitization
…PROTECTING THE HEALTH OF NIGERIANS
