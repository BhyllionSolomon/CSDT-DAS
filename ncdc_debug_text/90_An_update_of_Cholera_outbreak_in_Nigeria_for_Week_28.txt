TITLE: CHOLERA OUTBREAK IN NIGERIA
SERIAL NUMBER: 08
EPI-WEEK: 28
DATE: 18 July 2018
HIGHLIGHTS
§ The multi-agency cholera emergency operations centre (EOC) at NCDC continues to coordinate
the response.
§ Six States (Adamawa, Bauchi, Katsina, Zamfara, Plateau and Niger) are still reporting cases.
There is however a decline in the number of cases reported in Plateau, Kano and Adamawa
(Table 1).
§ In week 28 (9 -15 July); 367 new suspected cases were reported from six states- Adamawa (42),
Bauchi (56), Katsina (68), Niger (14), Plateau (11) and Zamfara (176) including four new deaths in
two States Katsina (2) and Zamfara (2).
§ This week, twelve (12) samples were tested. Of these, twelve (12) were positive.
§ Completed first round of OCV campaign targeting 378,815 persons in 21 wards in Adamawa state
EPI-Summary
§ As at 18th July 2018, a total of 16,892 suspected cases including 201 deaths (CFR: 1.19%) have
been reported from 17 States (Adamawa, Anambra, Bauchi, Borno, FCT, Gombe, Jigawa, Ebonyi,
Kaduna, Kano, Katsina, Kogi, Nasarawa, Niger, Plateau, Yobe, and Zamfara) since the beginning of
2018.
§ No new cases reported in the last four weeks in Anambra, Nasarawa, Borno and Yobe States
§ 51.6 % of the cases are aged 1 -14 years; 1-4years (27.1%) and 5-14years (24.5%)
§ Male/female ratio of cases is approximately 1:1 (50.1% male and 49.9% female)
§ Overall, there is a general declining trend in the number of cases reported except in Zamfara and
Katsina states.

RESPONSE ACTIVITIES
Coordination
§ Response is being coordinated by NCDC through an EOC in collaboration with NPHCDA, Federal Ministry of
Water Resources (FMWR), WHO, UNICEF, MSF, AFENET, UMB and US-CDC
§ Stakeholders’ meeting on the mid-long term interventions aimed at cholera control in Nigeria held on 19
July; consensus was reached and prioritization of cholera hotspots LGAs.
§ EOC meetings hold in most affected states to coordinate the outbreak response
§ Onsite and offsite support to all affected states
Surveillance
…PROTECTING THE HEALTH OF NIGERIANS
§ Active case search with daily update of line list is ongoing in affected States
Case management
§ 20 bed capacity Cholera Treatment Unit (CTU) opened in Plateau state (Jos North) with support from MSF
§ Cases are being treated at designated health facilities or Cholera Treatment Centres (CTCs) in the affected
States supported by partners
§ Official closing of the CTC in Bauchi State was done on the 16th of July, 2018. Running of the CTC was
supported by MSF France.
WaSH
§ WaSH activities ongoing in affected states
o 3296 persons sensitized in 930 households in Maiha LGA, Adamawa state
§ Sensitization of officers managing IDP camps in Gombe and Borno state
Laboratory Surveillance
§ Samples are being tested with Rapid Diagnostic Test kits (RDTs) in the field and confirmed by stool culture.
§ Total number of samples tested in week 28: 12
o Number positive by RDT: 12
o Number positive by culture: 0
Risk communications
§ Radio jingles in English and pidgin ongoing in affected states
§ IEC material printed for onward distribution to states
§ Sensitization messages provided in settlements within Mubi North and Mubi south LGA of Adamawa
state
Logistics and Vaccine
§ Cholera kits sent to Gombe state
§ Forecasted cholera commodities to be distributed to states for the next one month
§ First round of OCV campaign completed in parts of Maiha, Mubi North, and Mubi South LGAs of Adamawa
state
§ Post vaccination survey was conducted in Bauchi State supported by MSF France. The outcome of the
survey will be shared as soon as data analysis is completed
